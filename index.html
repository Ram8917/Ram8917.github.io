January 19, 2026 For doing it or for looking good while doing it ?

Topic: The photo got clicked!

So today I was dealing with the same feeling of not wanting to pick up my course material or anything that requires some activation energy. I got fed up and decided that this is where things would change and hence came up with an idea to completely change my digital environment and ways to train my brain into the direction I want to. Along with that I came up with this idea of creating a vision board for the next 1 year and for distant future. I used gemini to do so. I gave it my picture and defined clearly the kind of pictures I want in my vision board and it came up with a picture of me getting the Best MS thesis award from the director and me delivering a very nice talk on my MS thesis work. I felt "Ye to ho gaya"|"It is done" as in these thigns which I wanted to do are done. I can see myself get these and hence the picture I would have asked papa mummy to click has already been taken and it looks perfect. So, now what ? Hmmmmmm this made me think about why exactly do I want to have those situations in my life. To get a good photo which I can upload on the social media or to experience the happiness that comes after working very hard continuously ? So my friend, the picture has been clicked, now do it for the sake of the task.

January 20, 2026 Enjoyed with Parents

Topic: Care when nobody is looking!

Today after years I went to have darshan of Chaturshringi Devi. Her temple has been heavily renewed. I missed seeing her for a long time. Today when mummy said that we are taking/ wasting your time right ? I said that I wanted to go to Chaturshringi Temple and then you people look for yourselves. Didi really scolded me very rightly to say that why don't you finish you work when you are alone and when no one is disturbing you. What the hell is this behaviour? We become what we allow ourselves to become. If you really care for your family and parents, care for them even when they don't know whether you are caring or not.

August 14, 2026 Let us start this again

Be awake and observe your life

I am careful, concerned, responsible, conscious enough to realise that my actions happen once. Whatever I do in my life, however I choose to spend each and every breath of my life, there is something inside me which keeps telling me/ making me aware that whether or not I should do something or not. Every moment I keep thinking of what do I want to do seriously, this is something I should really invest my time on, woah the end product looks so cool and I want to myself create that end product, I want to feel similarly fulfilled, maybe I can get fulfilled if I create music like Kunfaya or Tum tak or maybe just alaap like a maestro and I myself get dissolved in that - forget myself in the process..... I think I will get shaanti if I make a room which looks like an investigator's room with chartpapers, newspapers, quotes and so on and whoever sees my room feels in awe about it, I feel I want to be able to write Hindi and English poetry such that it wrenches the strings in peoples' heart and they start crying..... I want to create music which moves people to become more more karunamayii, which can generate peoples' emotions, I want to get involved in movies which can make people feel every emotion on the screen .... I want to find/ think and explore wonderful things about the nature and climate and let people know how magnificent our planet is ----- I want to be able to dance well so that I can do a performance in my sister's wedding - such that she could never expect from me and she starts crying - maybe make my own music, song and choreography.... I want to write letters of expressing my love to my parents ... I want to express my feelings for my crushes to them through letters ... I want to be able to feel intimidated and blushed when a person whom I admire and like says something good about me ... I want to be able to be able to feel what it means to love something or someone so much that it makes one bear the unimaginable .... I want to be able to feel how it feels when your child calls you Papa - your son calls you phew phew and you do chandpain, kandpai, kandchain, when your daughter hugs you and tells you that you are the most dearest to he- how she feels safe around me ---- I want to be someone's home - a person with whom someone feels at home - calm---- I want my parents to be happy, if for that I need to help them learn to learn or become physically and mentally resilient - I want to spend my time thinking how they feel - how they think about things - how they imagine things to be in the world - what are their responsibilities - what things they have to take care of and so on ---- I want to know what fears them the most ---- I want to know what they think is meant by the world and life........ I want to know what fulfills them ---- I want to start thinking on my own instead of being a imitation machine - I want to do all of it - in its entirety I can't deny that whatever Sadhguru said about us humans - our brains wanting to get to the ulimate at everything - is because something inside of us wants to expand - I don't know - I want to sit with myself and feel what is happening with me - I want to sit in dark rooms and meditate for hours and so on.........
